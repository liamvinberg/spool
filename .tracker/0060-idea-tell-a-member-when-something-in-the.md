## Question

What tells a member that something changed on a team project, beyond what's already decided? Decided so far: a teammate's save shows up in place with the changed mark and a short toast on the read-only canvas; the phone navigator's recent list shows who saved what; a set-aside save puts a mark on each frame that renders the file, and a summary after coming back offline (Decide what happens when two writers change the same file (#17)).

Open:

- Whether anything reaches a member who isn't looking: email, the Mac app's menu bar item, a system notification.
- Whether a share's maker hears when someone else stops it (Prototype signing in and running a team (#44) handed this here).
- Whether admins hear about seat changes, a failed payment, or the 14 days' grace starting when a second editor joins (Decide what a team pays for (#20)).
- Whether a member hears that their role changed or that they were removed, beyond the daemon's own notice.
- Whether an edit-beats-delete resurrection tells the person who deleted it, and how (#17 says they're told; the channel isn't drawn).

None of the specs is blocked on this. Each ships the notices already decided, and this adds to them.
