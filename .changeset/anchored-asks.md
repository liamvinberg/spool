The agent's questions and requests for permission now open out of the work they are about. A request opens out of the turn's status line as "Waiting on you", with the agent's reason first, what it wants to run or edit one click away, and Allow, Allow for this chat and Deny. A question whose answers are frames the turn made turns its grid of pictures into numbered choices: press one to answer. A question about anything else is a quiet card at the end of the chat. A question that takes several answers has checkboxes and a Send button. You can still type your own answer, and Enter never approves anything. Once answered, each folds to one line, like "Allowed: edit src/theme.ts" or "You picked Timeline". With the agent panel closed, the question stands on the canvas under its frame, or under the place kept for it before it exists, and the agent's icon shows it is waiting on you.

On the canvas, a small square now shows where each agent is working and what it is doing: writing a new frame, reading one, changing a block, taking a screenshot or deleting. It dims when the agent pauses and leaves when the turn ends. Names appear when more than one agent is working.
