Teams come to Home. Once you're signed in, a switcher at the top of Home's sidebar moves between your own projects and each team you're in, and New team… starts one when your account may. A team has Projects, People and Settings for admins. On People you invite people by email with a role, change a role or remove someone, and leave a team. Settings holds the team's logo, name and address, and deleting it. An invite to your address shows as a line above your projects with Join and Decline. A team you only watch opens on spool.page in your browser.
