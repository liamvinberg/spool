---
"spool.page": patch
---

A team project's frames now get their pictures on spool.page. A Mac on another version of spool than spool.page had every picture it sent turned away, and pictures taken before a project joined its team never went up at all. Now each frame's picture is named by its files alone, so any version can send it, and when spool starts watching a team project it asks spool.page which pictures it has and sends the ones it is missing, or takes the ones nobody has taken yet. A shared page of a project of your own sends the pictures this Mac already has too, rather than waiting for the next change.
