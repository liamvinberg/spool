Who's here on a team canvas now sits at the canvas's top right instead of in the tab bar, so switching tabs no longer shifts the tabs. Press the faces to open the list of who's here, where each person is and how long they've been idle, then press a row to follow them. A team project's tab shows a dot for each person in it while you're on another tab, up to three.
