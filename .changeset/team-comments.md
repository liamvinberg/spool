Team projects have comments. Press C for the Comment tool, or hold C to borrow it, and click a frame or the empty field to put a pin there. Type and press Enter, and everyone on the team sees it on their canvas right away. Click any pin to read the comment and its replies and to reply. Pins show the author's initial in their colour. The canvas keeps the last comments it heard so they still show offline, but a new comment waits until the project is connected again. Older versions of spool can no longer sync with spool.page and need to update.
