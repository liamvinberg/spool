Adjust the space between a flex container's children on the canvas. A selected single-line row or column wears a band over each gap the layout can honestly name, and dragging one reflows every use of that source while the pointer is down — a row changes its column gap, a column its row gap, and a reversed or right-to-left layout grows the way it flows. A gap written on the project's spacing scale steps along that scale and one written in pixels steps in pixels; nothing is renamed on the way. Clicking a band opens the same exact value and scale the rail's own spacing fields offer, with one deliberate way off the scale into a pixel count. Letting go saves once and leaves one Undo entry, and Escape, a lost pointer, a window that loses focus or a scrolled canvas puts every preview back and saves nothing. Wrapped lines, distributed alignment, margins, transforms, other ambiguous geometry and gaps too thin to aim at draw no band at all and stay editable in Properties.
