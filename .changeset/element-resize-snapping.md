"spool.page": minor
---

Resizing an element now snaps to what is around it. Drag a handle and the edge you are holding catches on a neighbour's edge or middle, or on the inside of the box it sits in, when it comes within six pixels of one, and a thin red line shows what it lined up with. Hold Cmd (Ctrl on Windows) to switch that off for as long as you need it. The line is only drawn when the element really landed there, so a size the page rounds off or refuses leaves you the size you dragged and no line at all.
