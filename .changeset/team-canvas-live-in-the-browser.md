A team project's canvas in the browser is live. A teammate's save shows up in place, with the changed mark and a short toast saying who saved what, and a frame you are playing never moves under you: the next screen you walk to is the new version. Covers stand in for frames until they draw.

A team project's link at spool.page now opens it in spool when spool is open on your Mac, and offers to look in the browser otherwise. Editors get Open in spool on the browser canvas; viewers never see it.

Your Mac now sends the covers it takes of a team project's frames to spool.page, once for each version of a frame, so teammates see them in the browser.
