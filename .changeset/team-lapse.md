When a team has no paid time left, its local copies stop syncing without ending, and the folder, its link and git stay as they were. spool says once that the team has gone local, and the copy syncs again by itself within a minute of the team paying, sending what was made meanwhile as ordinary saves. On the team's Home, admins get one dialog to choose who stays an editor and pay, and everyone else a line saying the team has gone local, or that it isn't paid for yet when it never had time. Starting a project in such a team, moving one in or sharing from it says that an admin can pay for it in Settings › Billing.
