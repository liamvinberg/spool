When a team has no paid time left, its local copies stop syncing without ending: the folder, its link and git stay as they were, spool says once that the team has gone local, and the copy syncs again by itself once the team pays, sending what was made meanwhile as ordinary saves. On the team's Home, admins get one dialog to choose who stays an editor and pay, and everyone else a line saying the team has gone local.
