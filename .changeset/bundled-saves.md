A team project now sends changes made together as one bundle instead of one file at a time. A cleanup that deletes thousands of files reaches your team in seconds instead of half an hour, and your teammates see it land all at once. A bundle counts as one save toward the team's per-minute and monthly limits. This needs the spool.page that takes bundles; against an older one, spool sends one file at a time as before.
