**The agent's trail.** While your agent works, the canvas's top right names the frame it is writing, with an arrow that points the way to it. Press it to follow along: the canvas goes to each frame as it is written, and a pan or Esc lets go. When the turn ends it says how many frames it made for ten minutes, and a press lists them so you can jump to each.
