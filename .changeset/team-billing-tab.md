A team's Settings has two tabs, General and Billing. Billing is for admins: it says when the trial ends and that paying during it charges nothing until then, chooses who to pay for (admins and editors are seats) and monthly or yearly, goes to Stripe Checkout, and once paid shows the plan, the next charge and the card with Manage billing, saying there is no charge until the trial ends when paid for during it. spool.page/<team>/settings/billing opens it. Deleting a team with paid time says its subscription is cancelled at once.
