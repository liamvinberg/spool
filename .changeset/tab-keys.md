The Mac app has tab keys. ⌘W closes whatever is open on top, then the tab, and on Home the window. ⌘⇧W always closes the window, and ⌘⇧T brings back the tabs you closed, each in its old place and on the page and view you left. ⌘1 is Home, ⌘2 to ⌘8 the tabs after it and ⌘9 the last one. Ctrl+Tab and ⌘⇧] go to the next tab, Ctrl+⇧Tab and ⌘⇧[ to the one before, and ⌘T goes to Home. They work inside an entered frame too, and the File menu and a new Tab menu show them. In a browser tab the browser keeps these keys for its own tabs.
