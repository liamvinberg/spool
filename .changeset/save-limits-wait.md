"spool.page": patch

Moving a project into a team no longer stops on spool.page's save limits, and its files no longer count against your saves for the month. When a team project saves faster than spool.page takes saves, your changes now wait and go up by themselves instead of pausing sync, and a wait longer than a few seconds shows how many changes are left and about how long. spool tells you once when you reach 24,000 of your 30,000 saves to a project in a month, and sync paused at the month's cap now says the date it starts again.
