"spool.page": patch

Moving a big project to a team no longer fails when spool.page pauses saves for a minute or the connection drops. The move waits and carries on, and the move sheet shows how many files are up and why it is waiting. Reopening the sheet during a move picks it up where it is. A move that stops on a limit that won't lift, like a full project or the month's saves, now says so, and says when the month's saves come back.
