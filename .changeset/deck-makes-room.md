**Decks make room as you drag.** On a deck page a slide you pick up lifts, and the slides around it move apart to open a gap where it will land, the way icons make room in the Mac's Dock. The gap follows your hand, stays put while your hand rests, and turns round smoothly when you change your mind; let go and the slide lands in it once. A frame dropped onto a slide no longer swaps the two: it goes in at the gap. A long talk stays on one line. Fitting a deck page shows at least five slides across but never draws a slide narrower than 120 pixels, and holding a slide near the side of the window scrolls the talk under it. A slide picked in Pages comes into view.
