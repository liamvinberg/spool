Breaking: the sharing beta's publishing is gone. `spool cloud publish`, `stop`, `list`, `status`, `invite` and `revoke` are removed, and so are `spool build`, `spool check --entry` and `spool flows --entry`. Frames no longer have share links on the canvas or in the player. To share, right-click a page in the Pages panel and choose Share…. No page is refused for navigation spool can't read. An exported `links` object is no longer required for anything. Spool still updates the frame names in it when frames move.
