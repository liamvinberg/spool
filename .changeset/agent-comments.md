Agents can read and answer a team project's comments. `spool comments` lists the open comments, filtered by page, frame or share, and `spool comments show` prints one with its replies. Each comes as one block saying who wrote it, where it points in the source, and whether the frame changed since. It works offline from the last comments spool heard and says how old they are. Your agent can also reply, resolve, reopen and add comments, which go out as yours and show as "Ana's agent". On a comment's card, Send to agent puts the comment in the agent box. Nothing runs until you send, and Enter alone means deal with it. If the frame changed since, the agent also gets the picture of the frame from when the comment was made.
