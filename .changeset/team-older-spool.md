A team project's local copy on a Spool too old for its team now pauses instead of retrying in silence: the Sync box says "Update Spool to keep syncing with <team>", with the Mac app's update or the npm command, your files stay editable, and what you change goes up once Spool is updated. The next `spool` command in that project says the same. Spool now accepts teammates one sync protocol behind, and a team project's local copy moves to a newer design/ format only after it has caught up with the team, sending the result to teammates as ordinary saves.
