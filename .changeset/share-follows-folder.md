**Shares follow their folders.** A folder you share now takes in the pages added under it later, so the client sees them once they settle. The person you share with opens straight on what you shared: one folder is their top, called by its name, and several items sit side by side by their own names, with the parent's name in grey when two share a name. They never see the project's name, the folders above what you shared, or full paths in the player's bar.
