Added Settings › Experimental: one switch per feature still being tried, each with what it does and its trade-off, and a search over them. Fast refresh, Frame resolution and Element tree are off until you turn them on; Knobs is in beta and on, with a switch to turn it off. A change shows after a reload, which the section offers. `spool experiments` lists them and `spool experiments on <id>` or `off <id>` switches one. The switches live under `experimental` in `~/.spool/config.json`; an `experiments` list written by hand before still turns its features on.
