A turn in the agent rail now shows the frames it touched as a grid of pictures, with one status line under it. While designers work, each one's frame has its place in the grid and on the canvas from the moment it starts, and the line says how many are working and how many are done. A frame the agent is writing fills in line by line before it lands. Every step the turn took is still there: press the status line to open them. When the turn ends the line reads how long it took.

A turn now lasts until the designers it started have finished, rather than ending when the agent first answers, so their frames are no longer cut off. Stop ends them too. A frame the agent deletes can be brought back with Put back.
