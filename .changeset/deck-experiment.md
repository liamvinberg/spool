**Decks, as an experiment.** Turn on `spool experiments on deck` and a page holding a `deck.json` becomes a talk: spool lays its slides out in a row, in the order the file lists them, and keeps the other frames on the page in a grid below. A slide can reveal itself in steps with `ui.beats(["title", "price"])` and say it holds something to use while presenting with `ui.live()`, both on the `spool` import. Until a talk can be played, a slide always shows its last step, so it looks complete everywhere. Each slide can keep the presenter's words in a `notes.md` beside it, one paragraph per step. In Pages a deck page lists its talk in order, a line, then its other frames: drag a row to reorder the talk, or across the line to add a frame to the talk or take one out. Properties shows a slide's size as every slide's and gives each step of a slide its own notes field. New presentation, in the Pages menu, makes a deck page holding five starter slides and asks the agent what the talk is about. `spool check` points out a deck that names a missing slide, steps that are not written out as a list, and a slide that imports its notes. `spool skill deck` teaches an agent how to write one.
