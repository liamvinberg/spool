**Decks, as an experiment.** Turn on `spool experiments on deck` and a page holding a `deck.json` becomes a talk: spool lays its slides out in a row, in the order the file lists them, and keeps the other frames on the page in a grid below. A slide can reveal itself in steps with `ui.beats(["title", "price"])` and say it holds something to use while presenting with `ui.live()`, both on the `spool` import. On the canvas a slide shows its last step, so it looks complete; presenting plays its steps in order. Each slide can keep the presenter's words in a `notes.md` beside it, one paragraph per step. In Pages a deck page lists its talk in order, a line, then its other frames: drag a row to reorder the talk, or across the line to add a frame to the talk or take one out. Properties shows a slide's size as every slide's and gives each step of a slide its own notes field. Double-click a slide, or press ↵ on it, and the canvas flies into it and plays the talk there with the presenting keys, all but the room's, and ? lists them; Esc flies back out, to where you were if the talk is still on that slide, or across the slide it reached, which stays selected. A talk presented from inside a slide lands the same way when it ends, and a row pressed in Pages leaves the slide first. ⌘ double-click still opens a slide's words to edit. New presentation, in the Pages menu, makes a deck page holding five starter slides and asks the agent what the talk is about. Present, from a slide's play button, a deck's row in Pages, ⌘↵ on a slide or ⇧⌘↵ from the first, turns spool's window into the presenter view (the clock, the slide, what the next key plays, the step's notes, and the talk as a bar per slide to click through) and opens the room's window, which shows the slides and nothing else and goes full screen on its first key or click. In the Mac app the room has no title bar and opens full screen on the display spool's window is not on, named in the presenter view, and X swaps the two screens; with one display, or once its display is unplugged, it is an ordinary window. B blacks the room, F holds it while you look ahead, Tab hands the keys to a live slide and Esc takes them back, then ends the talk on the slide it reached. `spool check` points out a deck that names a missing slide, steps that are not written out as a list, and a slide that imports its notes. `spool skill deck` teaches an agent how to write one, and `spool shot <slide> --beat 2` pictures a slide at an earlier step to check a reveal. Export, on a deck page or from its row in Pages, sends the talk as a PDF, one page per slide at its last step, or as one HTML file that plays it from disk: arrows step through it, F goes full screen and Esc leaves.
