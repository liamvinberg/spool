Sharing now lives in a Share button at the top right, next to help and settings. It shares the page you're on with a link by default, lets you pick more pages from a searchable list that stays one size however big the project is, and shows a QR code of the link so you can open the prototype on your phone straight away. The project's shares are listed in the same place, so the Shared button no longer comes and goes beside your tabs. Right-clicking a page and choosing Share… opens it on that page.
