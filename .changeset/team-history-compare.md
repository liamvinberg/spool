Compare a frame with how it stood in a team project's history. Open a stretch in the History pane and press Compare with now: the frame as it was at the end of that stretch stands where it was, labelled with its time, and today's slides aside, labelled now. It shows the frame's own files and the shared files they imported back then. A frame deleted since comes back dashed in its old place. A save that was set aside compares from under it with its own file. Hovering a stretch outlines the frames it touched in that person's colour. What is read is kept in a bounded cache on this Mac, never in `design/`.
