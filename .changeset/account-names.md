People in a team show by their name instead of their email: on People, in the note that someone's save didn't apply, on shares, when setting up a team project and when moving one to another team. Someone who hasn't given a name shows as the part of their address before the @, the same everywhere. You can set or change your own name on the Account page, and everyone sees the new one at once. Your email still shows on People, on your Account page and in your sidebar menu.
