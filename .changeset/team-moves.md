Move a team project to another team, out of its team, or into a checkout, from the app or with `spool move`, which says what the move does and asks first (`--yes` once the person said yes). Moving to another team keeps the same project, its history and shares; this Mac's copies take the new `spool.json` in one commit on the current branch (a copy kept just on this Mac moves to the new team's folder), and teammates' copies follow its new address and keep syncing until they pull. Moving out catches the copy up, then one commit puts `design/` back in git and takes `spool.json` out; everyone else's copy ends, out of git until they pull. Moving a copy in no repo into a checkout moves `design/` in and commits `spool.json` there, and it keeps syncing without uploading again. Whenever spool puts `spool.json` in a repo (Move to team, Move into a checkout, `spool init` of a team project in a checkout, Get it into a checkout), it adds "design/ is a spool canvas: run `spool skill` before working there." to the checkout's AGENTS.md, else CLAUDE.md, else a new AGENTS.md, once.
