**Frames keep their size.** A frame now plays at exactly the size it was drawn, centred in the window, instead of being stretched to the window's height, so a phone frame looks like a phone. A long page keeps its width and scrolls rather than shrinking to a sliver. The bar always says the size the frame plays at, like `390 × 844` or `1440 × 944 · 65%`, and a click on it switches to the window's own size. A thin white line that could show beside a dark frame on some displays is gone.
