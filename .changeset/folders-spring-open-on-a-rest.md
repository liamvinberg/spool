A drag through the pages rail now opens only the folder it rests on. A shut page used to open the instant a drag arrived at it, so carrying a folder to the bottom of a long tree unfolded every folder on the way and moved the gap being aimed at out from under the pointer. Resting on one for 450ms opens it, an arc around its chevron counts the rest out, and a folder merely crossed is left exactly as it was.
