Four things the hand got wrong. A double-click on a block inside the element you are holding picks that block; only an element that is already held and has words of its own opens them to type in, so a heading inside a section is a rung on the way down rather than a text edit you did not ask for. The frame you are dragging in keeps animating for as long as the drag lasts, so a shader resized by a handle redraws instead of going black, while every other frame on the canvas stays still. Undo and redo point at the element the step was about again, with the ring read fresh off its new box, and let the pick go when the step took the element away. And scrubbing a number in the rail works in the Mac app: the drag runs on how far the hand moved whether or not the pointer lock was granted, and the app now grants it.
