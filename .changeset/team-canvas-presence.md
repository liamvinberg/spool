See who else is on a team project's canvas. Each teammate's pointer shows in their own colour with their name, which fades a moment after they stop. An idle teammate's pointer dims, and one who leaves fades out. While someone is inside a frame, their name sits beside that frame's name, and a frame someone is moving shows their colour around it. Their faces are at the top right of the window: press one to follow their view, and press esc or move your own view to stop. Nothing about where people are is saved to disk.
