**Finder on the path.** The frame finder has a door at the end of the page path: move toward the top of the canvas and a magnifier with ⌘K comes up beside the names. It, ⌘K or / opens the finder right there, with a picture of every frame, the one you picked shown large, and a small map of its page. It also finds a frame by its page, and from three letters by the words on it.
