When you pull a commit that moved a project's design/ into a Spool Cloud team you aren't an editor of, its cover on Home now says where design/ went and who moved it, instead of going empty. Opening it shows who moved it in which commit and what you can do: ask them to invite you, open it in the browser as a viewer, or sign in. Frames you never committed are listed and go up by themselves once you're made an editor. Remove from Home hides the project. The same sheet appears over the project's canvas if it was open when the pull landed.
