Your account at spool.page/account now opens inside Home, beside your team, from your email at the foot of the sidebar. It lists your Macs and passkeys the way Settings lists things, and you can revoke a Mac, add or remove a passkey and sign out there. Someone in no team yet lands there too, with a way to start one. On a Mac, the menu item is now called Account.
