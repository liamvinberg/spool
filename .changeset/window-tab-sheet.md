The window's tabs start where the left side ends, and follow it as you resize or close it. The open tab is one sheet that slides from tab to tab and flows into the canvas below it. When the bar fills up, tabs shrink down to their icon instead of scrolling away, and the open tab keeps its name. After you close a tab with the pointer, the others keep their width until the pointer leaves the bar, so you can close several in a row from the same spot.
