A reference sent from the Chrome extension now does more of what the site did: videos that were playing keep playing, what the site's script changed as you hovered a part (a card lighting up, its video starting) happens again under the pointer, and clicking a link leaves the reference as it is instead of turning it white. A part picked from a page no longer comes out blank when the site hid it by what stood beside it, and what it drew behind itself stays in sight. A page with a large WebGL hero sends far less.
