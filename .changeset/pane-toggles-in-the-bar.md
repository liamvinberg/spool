The pane icons moved from the rails at the window's edges into the window bar. Pages sits at the far left before Home, Properties and Agent at the far right after help and settings, and a pane moved to the other side takes its icon to the other end of the bar. Panes now sit flush against the window edge with a quiet title row, and their buttons show when you point at the row. Drop a dragged pane among the icons in the bar to move it there. Pages no longer shows the "folder switches page" line under its tree.
