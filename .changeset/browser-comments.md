"spool.page": minor

Members of a team can now comment on its projects in the browser at spool.page. Press C or pick the Comment tool, click a frame or the canvas, and write; hold C to place one pin and go back to the Pointer. The pins, cards, replies, @mentions and the ⋯ menu work as they do on the Mac, and new comments from teammates show up live. A Comments button beside Shared opens the list in a panel on the right, and clicking a comment flies to its pin, on its own page. A Copy link opens spool.page right on that comment. On the Mac and in the browser, cards now grow out of their pin and shrink back into it, and pins pop in.
