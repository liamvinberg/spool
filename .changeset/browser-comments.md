"spool.page": minor
---

Members of a team can now comment on its projects in the browser at spool.page. Press C or pick the Comment tool, click a frame or the canvas, and write; hold C to place one pin and go back to the Pointer. The pointer becomes a pin, and the element under it is outlined as on the Mac. The pins, cards, replies, @mentions and the ⋯ menu work as they do on the Mac, and new comments from teammates show up live. A card says when its frame was edited since, and Compare shows the frame as it was then beside how it is now. A Comments button beside Shared counts the whole project's open comments and opens the list in a panel on the right; clicking a comment flies to its pin, on its own page. A Copy link opens spool.page right on that comment. Once a team has gone local, the browser drops its Comment tool and its cards drop their reply field.
