"spool.page": minor
---

Members of a team can now comment on its projects in the browser at spool.page. Press C or pick the Comment tool, click a frame or the canvas, and write; hold C to place one pin and go back to the Pointer. The pointer becomes a pin, and the element under it is outlined as on the Mac. The pins, cards, replies, @mentions and the ⋯ menu work as they do on the Mac, and new comments from teammates show up live. A card says when its frame was edited since, and Compare shows the still it kept beside the frame now. A Comments button beside Shared counts the whole project's open comments and opens the list in a panel on the right; clicking a comment flies to its pin, on its own page. A Copy link opens spool.page right on that comment. Once a team has gone local, its comments can still be read but not written: cards on the Mac and in the browser drop their reply field, resolve and edit, and the browser drops its Comment tool too. On the Mac and in the browser, cards now grow out of their pin and shrink back into it, and pins pop in once.
