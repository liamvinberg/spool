**Who's here, honestly.** A teammate now reads as active, unfocused, away or gone. Active means their spool window has focus and they changed something in the last 2 minutes. Unfocused means spool is open behind another window, or 2 minutes went by without a change. Away means their canvas is hidden, or 5 minutes went by. After 30 minutes away they are gone. Someone with spool open in the background no longer shows as here for hours.

Everyone on your page stays on your canvas. An unfocused pointer rests where they left it, faint. An away one folds into a small dot that says how long. A pointer off your screen shows as their face at the nearest edge, and pressing it follows them. The faces of who's here now sit right after the page path at the top left, and pressing them opens the list of who is on this page, who is elsewhere, who is away, and you. The same goes for the canvas on spool.page.
