The team canvas in the browser stays smooth on big projects. Frames are drawn from their covers on the GPU, as on the Mac, and a frame's live screen is only loaded once it is large enough to read near where you're looking, a couple at a time, showing its cover or its name until it has arrived. Moving the canvas pauses the live screens. A frame a teammate saved shows live until its new cover lands. It works on a share's own address too, and in a browser that can't draw on the GPU.
