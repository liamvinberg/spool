A frame's still now draws at the shape it was photographed, never stretched to the box it stands in. Resizing a frame gives it a new footprint seconds before the fresh capture lands, and through those seconds — the drag included — the old picture used to smear across the new size. It now sits contained in the frame's corner over the placeholder surface, an honest picture of what the frame was until the new one arrives; a still whose shape matches its frame fills it edge to edge exactly as before.
