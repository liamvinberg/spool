Sign in to spool.page from the foot of Home, or with `spool login`. Both open your browser, where you continue with Google, an email code or a passkey, and the Mac stays signed in while you use it. The account menu opens your Macs and passkeys on spool.page and has Sign out of this Mac, which `spool logout` also does. A sign-in from an earlier version is not carried over, so sign in once more after updating.
