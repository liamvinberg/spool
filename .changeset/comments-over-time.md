Comments follow their frames. Renaming or moving a frame or a page keeps its comments on it. If a frame is edited after a comment was made, the comment's card says so, and Compare shows the frame as it was then beside how it is now. Deleting a frame leaves its comments where their pins stood, with dashed pins and a note in the card; bringing the frame back puts them back on it. When a team goes local, its comments can still be read but not written. The sheet that takes a project out of its team says how many comments are deleted with it.
