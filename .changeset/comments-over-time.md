Comments follow their frames. Renaming or moving a frame or a page keeps its comments on it. If a frame's source is edited after a comment was made, the comment's card and its row in the Comments tab say so (a rename, a move or a spool update doesn't count), and Compare shows the frame as it was then beside how it is now. Deleting a frame leaves its comments where their pins stood, with dashed pins and a note in the card; bringing the frame back puts them back on it. When a team goes local, its comments can still be read but not written: cards drop their reply field, resolve and edit.
