**Agents in your terminal.** Claude Code, Codex or pi working in a terminal now shows on the canvas the way spool's own agent does. It shows which frames it reads and edits, when it waits on you, and what it changed once done. spool asks once to connect an agent it notices. You can also connect or disconnect one any time in Settings, or with `spool hooks`. The hooks only listen: they add nothing to the agent's context and never slow it down.
