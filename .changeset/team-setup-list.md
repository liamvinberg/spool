A new team's admins get a setup list on its Projects page, the same in the app and at spool.page/<team>: name the team, pay first when the account's trial was used, invite people or skip, get spool on your Mac, and move a project in. On a team that already pays, each editor's invite row says what one more seat costs. Once the last step is done the list goes for good with a toast, and a link from spool.page can hand a team alone over to spool on this Mac.
