spool is now under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-MIT) instead of MIT. You can still use it for your own work, at a company too, and read, change and share the code. The one new limit is selling a product that competes with spool. Each release becomes MIT two years after it comes out, and versions up to 0.39.0 stay MIT.
