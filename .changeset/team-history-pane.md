Added a History pane to team projects' canvases, beside Properties, Agent and Comments, opened and closed with ⇧⌘H. Right-click a frame and pick History… for that frame's saves, including those that reached it through the shared files it imports; pick History… on a project's tab for the whole project's. Saves are grouped by day and by stretch of one person's work, spaced by the quiet between them, and can be narrowed to one person. Picking a stretch shows the files it touched and whose save stood over any that were set aside. A project moved in from git ends its history with when, by whom and in which commit it moved.
