Inside the Mac app, the canvas now shows the app's own update in the same pill that offers the npm one: Spool is out, Update, a download that counts up, Restarting. The app checks for a release by itself, ten seconds after it opens and once a day after that, so nobody has to open the menu to find out. When it cannot update itself it says why and hands over the dmg instead.
