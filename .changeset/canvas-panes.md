The canvas window has panes. Pages, Properties and Agent each have an icon on a rail at the left or right edge. Click an icon to show or hide its pane, and ⌥-click to show only that one. Drag a pane by its header or its icon to stack it under another or move it to the other side. Right-click an icon to move it or take it off the rail. ⌘B and ⌘⌥B collapse the left and right sides, ⌘⇧E, ⌘⇧I and ⌘⇧K show Pages, Properties and Agent, and ⌘K finds the layout commands. The layout is remembered per browser. Layouts saved by earlier versions are not carried over, so the panes start in the default place once.
