The canvas window has panes, as tabs on either side of the canvas. Pages stands on the left, and Properties and Agent share the right as two tabs, Properties showing. Click a tab to switch to its pane; a pane behind another tab keeps its draft and scroll. A pane's own buttons show in its tab row when you point at it: Pages has a new page button and a ⋯ menu with Collapse all, and Agent has New chat, with the chat's title on a line under the tab. Drag a tab onto another tab row to move it there, or onto the top or bottom half of a pane to split the side, up to three groups a side; Esc cancels a drag, and a drop that does not fit says why. Right-click a tab to move it to the other side, split it below or merge it into the group above. The button at a side's outer end closes it to a narrow rail of its panes' icons; click an icon to open the side on that pane. Agent's running and unread marks show on its tab and its rail icon. Drag a side's edge to resize it. ⌘B and ⌘⌥B open and close the left and right sides, ⌘⇧E, ⌘⇧I and ⌘⇧K open Pages, Properties and Agent, and ⌘K finds Show, Move, Split and Reset layout. When the window gets too narrow, the side you used least recently closes to its rail. The window bar is the same on Home and in a project, with help and settings at its right end. The layout is remembered per browser; layouts saved by earlier versions are not carried over, so the panes start in the default place once. Pages no longer shows a page count or the "folder switches page" line under its tree.
