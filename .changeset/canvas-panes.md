The canvas window has panes. Pages, Properties and Agent each have an icon in the window bar, at the end of the bar on the side where the pane sits: Pages at the far left before Home, Properties and Agent at the far right after help and settings. Click an icon to show or hide its pane, and ⌥-click to show only that one. Panes sit flush against the window edge with a quiet title row, and their buttons show when you point at the row. Drag a pane by its title row to stack it under another, or drop it among the icons in the bar to move it to that side; its icon goes with it. Right-click an icon to move it or take it out of the bar. ⌘B and ⌘⌥B collapse the left and right sides, ⌘⇧E, ⌘⇧I and ⌘⇧K show Pages, Properties and Agent, and ⌘K finds the layout commands. The layout is remembered per browser. Layouts saved by earlier versions are not carried over, so the panes start in the default place once. Pages no longer shows the "folder switches page" line under its tree.
