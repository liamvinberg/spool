Presence stays light in a crowded team project. Pointers now go only to people on the same page, a tick at a time, and at most 50 are shown on a page, editors first. Someone you follow is always shown. Past the 50, your canvas says your pointer is hidden and stops sending it until a place frees up; your page still shows. Older and newer Spools and spool.page keep working together.
