Share pages with people outside your team. Right-click a page and choose Share… to show it, and any other pages you pick, to named people who sign in with their address, or to anyone with a fresh link of its own. The sheet warns about links that leave the pages you share, and never stops you. The Shared control at the top right lists a project's shares; open one to add or remove people, copy its link, see who made it and how often it was opened, or stop sharing, which ends their access at once.

People you share with see only those pages, as they last settled: when your agent finishes its turn, or after half a minute with no saves. A save that breaks a frame leaves them on the last version that compiled.

Projects of your own share the same way while you're signed in to spool.page: spool uploads only the files the shared pages are made of, and their covers.

In the browser, members see the project's shares in the same Shared control, and editors and admins manage them there.
