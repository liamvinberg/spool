"spool.page": patch
---

Playing a frame on spool.page is smoother. The frame grows out of the canvas once it has drawn, instead of as a blank page that fills in, and the canvas fades behind it as it opens and closes. Walking to another screen keeps the last one on show until the next has drawn. A link that names only a frame, like `?frame=shop%2Fcart`, opens on that frame's own page.
