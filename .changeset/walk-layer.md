The canvas draws every walk it knows about, not just the ones an arrow can reach. A walk that lands on another page now shows as a small tag docked to the frame that declares it, saying where it goes and on which page. Press it and you travel there: the canvas switches page, centres on the frame and selects it. A walk that lands nowhere shows the same way but stops short of its tag with a cross, and says why — the name nothing answers to, or the line spool could not read. Zoom out past the point where the words fit and each one shrinks to a stub on the frame's wall, so you can still see which frames leave the page and where it is broken. The threads button governs all of it, and while it is off it keeps a small dot whenever the page it is hiding has a walk that goes nowhere.
