The agent rail names a thread by what you asked. The plate over the log shows the open thread's ask, the marks of threads working or finished elsewhere, and a plus for a new thread. Pressing the ask drops a list of every thread over the log, each with its ask, the frames it wrote and its age, with a close on hover. The column of marks down the rail's edge and its hover flyout are gone, and the dock glyph is the one thing that shuts the panel.
