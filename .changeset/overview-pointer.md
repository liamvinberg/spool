With ⌥ held for the overview, the pointer picks too. Hover a frame to make it the target, click one to go there at once, or click empty canvas to go back. Clicks never reach a prototype while the overview is up, and the wheel zooms it out so you can reach any frame. The overview also drops its corner marks: the ring on the target is the one mark of where you are going.
