Team projects can be renamed now, from the tab or from Rename on their card, by the team's editors and admins. The team renames it for everyone, and teammates' copies follow by themselves. New project in a team opens with its name ready to type, as it does on your own Mac. An admin can delete a team project from its card with Delete from the team. It waits in the team's trash for 30 days. On a team project, Move to Trash is now Remove from this Mac, which says the team keeps it. An untitled team project you close without using is deleted by itself, as your own untitled projects are.
