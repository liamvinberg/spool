Decks hold still while you drag (experiment `deck`). A hand resting or trembling over a slide no longer flips the preview back and forth, a loose frame moves out of the way only once the frame you hold is over it, and a drop or an undo settles in one glide instead of jumping to its place, back, and on again. Undoing a change of the talk's size puts the slides straight back where they belong.
