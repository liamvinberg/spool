**Play at any size.** Pull the edge of a played frame to lay it out at another width or height. On the way it holds on the sizes of a phone, a tablet, a laptop, a desktop and the frame's own. The size on the bar now opens a list of sizes: As drawn, Window, Phone, Tablet, Laptop and Desktop. Rest on one to see the frame at it, and click to keep it. Double-click an edge to go back to the size the frame was drawn at. As drawn or Window is remembered for the next play; any other size lasts until you close it.
