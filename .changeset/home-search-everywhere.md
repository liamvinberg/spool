Home has one search for all your projects once you are in a team. It sits at the top of the sidebar and finds projects in your own list and in every team, including team projects that aren't on this Mac yet. Tabs narrow the results to one place. Under it, Recent lists every project you opened, whichever team it is in, and Home opens there. A team's Projects page now shows how many projects the team has and sorts them like your own. On spool.page, the same search finds projects in every team you're in.
