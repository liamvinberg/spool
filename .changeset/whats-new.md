**What's new.** The first time Spool opens on a new version, the update pill comes back as "Updated to Spool" and unfolds into what came with it: each highlight with its clip, then the rest of the new features, how many fixes there were, and a link to the full changelog. Step through with ← and →, and Esc puts it away. It shows once, and only for an update with new features; an update of fixes alone stays quiet. After that it waits in the help menu, under What's new, for as long as you are on that version. Spool reads it from spool.page/changelog, so with no connection it waits for the next time you open Spool.
