"spool.page": minor
---

`spool skill` takes several topics at once (`spool skill frames styling verbs`), and its overview is shorter: history and team projects moved into their own topics, and it now carries a short Playwright driver for clicking or typing in a frame before a screenshot, on spool's own browser. `spool url --raw` points at that driver. `spool status` and the project verbs warn when the daemon runs from a checkout that was deleted, a long team fetch in `spool open` says how far it got, and the advice about `spool stop --force` now says to retry first. The daemon no longer logs a cover spool.page already has a newer one of, and a reader that stops early, like `| head`, no longer makes the CLI print a stack.
