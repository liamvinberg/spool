Added Record, an experiment you turn on in Settings › Experimental. Right-click a frame and pick Record, or press the dot in the player's bar, and pick 16:9, 1:1 or the frame's own shape: the frame plays at that shape while you record, so what you see is what the clip holds. Press the time or Escape to stop, and an MP4 downloads once it has rendered, ready to post. The clip is rendered at 60 fps, so it plays smoothly whatever your machine was doing, with the frame's own sound and a Mac cursor that glides and hides when the pointer rests. It follows you when you walk to another frame, and recording stops by itself at a minute. `spool record <frame>` makes a clip of a frame that plays by itself, with `--seconds`, `--aspect` and `--out`.
