Added Record, an experiment you turn on in Settings › Experimental. Right-click a frame and pick Record, or press the dot in the player's bar, then use the frame as you would. Press the dot or Escape to stop, pick 16:9, 1:1 or the frame's own shape, and an MP4 downloads, ready to post. The clip is rendered afterwards at 60 fps, so it plays smoothly whatever your machine was doing, and it keeps the frame's own sound. It follows you when you walk to another frame, and recording stops by itself at a minute. `spool record <frame>` makes a clip of a frame that plays by itself, with `--seconds`, `--aspect` and `--out`.
