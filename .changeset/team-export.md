Admins can export a team from the Mac app: Export all in a team's Settings, or Export… on a team project's cover, writes each project into a folder you pick as a git repository with the team's history as commits, authored by whoever saved them, beside a `team.json` of the team's people and shares. On spool.page, Export opens the Mac app on the export, or offers the download. A deleted team stays in its admins' switcher until it is gone for good, with Export all on its page.
