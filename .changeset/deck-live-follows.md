**Live slides follow you.** What you do in a live slide while presenting now happens on the room's screen too: clicks, hover, scrolling, typing, drags and keys. A prototype works in a talk as it is written, its own state included, without keeping anything in `ui.state`. A room window that opens or reloads mid-talk catches up on where lists are scrolled, what fields hold and what has the focus.
