Going inside a frame no longer takes you further away. Zoom in on a long page to read it, double-click to make it live, and the camera stays exactly where you left it instead of pulling back to fit the whole frame on screen. Entering from a wider view still fits, as it always did.

⏎ on a frame you have since scrolled away from pans to it at the zoom you are already at, the way arriving on a walk does.
