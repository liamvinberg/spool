A team project's copy that stopped syncing, because the project was removed, you were removed or made a viewer, or the team went local, can now go back into git in one step. Choose Put design/ back in git on the copy's notice or its cover menu. It makes one commit on your current branch that adds design/ and takes spool.json out, and never pushes. A copy whose team went local stops following the team for good.
