Holding ⌥ no longer zooms out so far that every frame turns into a sliver. The overview steps back at most to half the zoom you were at, keeps that zoom while you move, and only pans to the frame you move to. A neighbour too far away to show gets a tag at the edge of the screen with its key and name, and its arrow still takes you there.
