Comments on team projects can now be resolved, reopened, edited and deleted. Each message has a ⋯ menu with Edit, Copy link, Mark as unread and Delete. Type @ to mention a teammate. A new Comments tab beside Properties and Agent lists every comment on the page or the whole project, filtered to unread or to the ones for you, and clicking one flies to its pin. ⌘⇧U opens it. What you have read follows you to your other Macs. Pins that crowd together merge into a count you can click open, and Shift+C hides or shows every pin. A page on the canvas shows how many comments are open inside it.
