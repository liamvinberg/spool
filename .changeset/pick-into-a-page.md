Picking into a page works the way it does in a design tool. In the edit tool a click takes the section under the pointer instead of the wrapper drawn over the whole frame, a double-click goes one level in, and a double-click on words opens them with the caret where you clicked. Enter goes in, Shift+Enter comes back out, Escape climbs. The edit tool never runs the prototype: not by double-click, not from the frame label, not on Enter, and picking it up while a frame is running steps out of it. While you type, only the words take the pointer, so the rest of the page stays the canvas's.

Clicks land where you aim them. The handles that turn and resize an element only appear on a box big enough to draw them, so a heading one line high no longer wears dead zones over its words, and the note saying why a change was refused takes no presses of its own. Text drawn across several lines is ringed line by line, and the ring follows the element after a resize. Every number in the rail is scrubbed from the field itself: press and drag and it keeps counting past the edge of the screen, on values named by the theme as much as on plain ones. After a change is saved the rail keeps its readings instead of going blank for a moment.
