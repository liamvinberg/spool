Teammates see each other in the browser too. A member looking at a team canvas at spool.page, a viewer or an editor away from their Mac, sees everyone's pointers, names, docked pills and faces as the Mac's canvas shows them, can press a face to follow someone, and appears on everyone else's canvas the same way. A frame they play counts as the frame they're inside. People you share pages with outside the team see nobody and appear nowhere.

On a phone, the navigator shows who is in each part: everyone on the canvas at the top, who is on a page or under it beside its row, and who is inside a frame on its cover.

On Home, a team project's cover says who is inside it right now, "sam is here" or "2 here", and keeps it current while Home is open.
