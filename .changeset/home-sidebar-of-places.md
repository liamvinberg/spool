Home lists every team you're in down its sidebar, in name order, with no menu to open: the team you're in slides open to show Projects, People and Settings, and its page is titled with its name. Your projects sits below a rule, with Open… beside New project. On the Mac each place has its own address (`/recent`, `/<team>`, `/<team>/people`, `/<team>/settings/billing`), so a reload keeps you there, back and forward move between places, and the next launch opens where you last were. The list of the app's own path segments ships as `APP_PATH_SEGMENTS` from `spool.page/sync-protocol`.
