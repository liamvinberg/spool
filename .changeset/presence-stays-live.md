Teammates on a team canvas now show up right away and stay shown. A Mac that slept or switched networks reconnects to the team within about half a minute instead of waiting minutes on a dead connection, and tries again every 15 seconds at most while offline. Someone sitting still on the canvas no longer vanishes for everyone else when their app reconnects to Spool in the background, and a canvas opened before its project finished syncing is shown to the team once it has. The same holds for a canvas open in the browser.
