Breaking: the Edit tool now selects the deepest element under the pointer with one click, and nothing you do with it reaches the page underneath. While it is on, animations on every live frame hold still and start again when you put it down. From a selected element, double-click or Enter edits its words or selects the children of a group, Esc or Shift-Enter selects the parent, Tab walks the siblings and ⌘A selects them all. Esc now saves the words you are typing, like Enter. A label beside the outline names the element. When a delete or a text edit can't be made, a note under the element says why and offers to ask the agent. The step-by-step selection and the breadcrumbs in the properties rail are gone.
