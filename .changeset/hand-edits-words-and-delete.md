Breaking: editing by hand on the canvas now changes an element's words and deletes elements, and nothing else. The properties rail no longer edits an element's classes, layout, picture, attributes or visibility, and the element resize, rotate and gap handles and the ⌥ spacing measurement are gone. Ask the agent for those changes; when a hand edit can't be made, the note on the element still offers to. With an element selected, the rail shows the size and position of the frame it is in.
