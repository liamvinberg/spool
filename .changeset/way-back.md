**A way back.** On spool.page, a canvas's path now starts with where it lives: your team's name takes you back to its projects, and a client's `shared` takes them to Shared with you. ⌘↑ climbs one step at a time, and going between Home and a canvas is instant, with no page load. Shared with you is a new place in Home that lists every canvas shared with you from outside your teams, grouped by who shared it, with a dot on what changed since you last looked. Signing in with no team lands there. Home's teams now sit in one box that opens a menu of your teams, on the Mac too.
