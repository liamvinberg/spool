Settings is now a page instead of a sheet over the canvas. It opens in place of the tab you are on, from the gear, from Settings on Home or with ⌘, (ctrl , elsewhere), and esc or Back takes you to exactly where you were. Its sections are the open project, This Mac, Appearance and Colours, each naming the file it writes. Appearance shows a large preview of the window that wears whichever theme you point at before you pick it.
