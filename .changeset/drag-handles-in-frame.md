Resize and gap handles follow the pointer again. A drag now changes the element in the frame on every sample with nothing sent to the daemon, so the box moves under your hand instead of a few times a second; snapping guides and the Shift and Option modifiers work as they did. Letting go saves once and takes one step back, and the frame you dragged in is not reloaded for its own save. A gap band moves only the axis it stands on. Escape, a lost pointer or a window that loses focus mid-drag puts the element back and writes nothing.
