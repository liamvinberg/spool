Sharing is quicker and quieter. Right-click a frame and choose Copy share link: the link lands on your clipboard as soon as it has an address, while the files keep uploading, and the next frame you share reuses the same people. The frame's label now says whether it is uploading, shared or changed since the link was updated. Pressing that label opens a small panel beside the frame for access, updates and stopping, replacing the dialog and the progress tray. The player's Share button opens the same panel.
